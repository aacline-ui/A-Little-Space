Meet the person and purpose behind A Little Space.

Who is behind the blog?

I’m Shelby Cline, the writer behind A Little Space. I created this personal website as part of a class assignment. It gives me a place to practice writing for the web and share what I’m learning.

What you’ll find here

This website brings together my blog posts and reflections. The Menu page helps you explore the site, and the Blog page is where you can find my latest writing.

What matters in this space

My goal is to make this website welcoming and easy to use. I value clear writing, thoughtful feedback, and respect for different perspectives. I hope each post gives readers something to think about and a reason to join the conversation.
